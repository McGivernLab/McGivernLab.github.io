An Integrated Framework For Understanding Non-Neural Intelligence

<img align="right" width="30%" alt="Templeton-logo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5b9471c8-a125-4fcf-9536-5c0fb65388c5" />

This project aims to deepen our understanding of the evolution of cognition by investigating how frameworks describing major transitions in cognition might be applied to non-neural systems. Such frameworks typically begin with the introduction of neurons and then move to increasing levels of neural complexity. This leave many questions concerning the development of non-neural capacities unanswered. The goal of this project is to clarify how similar forms of transition might be understood in non-neural cases, by considering both the architectural differences in information flow between neural and non-neural systems and the unique form of cognitive capacities found on the microscopic scales that non-neural creatures typically inhabit.

This project is generously funded by the Templeton World Charity Foundation, under their Diverse Intelligences scheme.
